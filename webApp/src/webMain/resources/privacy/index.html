LOOTTRACK LEGAL

PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: MAY 2024 • DEVELOPER: DIRZA AULIA

1. OVERVIEW

LootTrack is a video game deal aggregation and price tracking application built for Android and Web. We value your trust and are dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled within the application.

2. DATA COLLECTION & PRICE ALERTS

LootTrack does not collect, track, or sell personal identifiers, location data, contacts, or financial details.

When you voluntarily set a Price Alert in LootTrack, your email address and requested target price are transmitted securely to our notification infrastructure solely to send automated email alerts when game deals reach your target price.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & APIS

LootTrack retrieves deal listings, stores, and currency conversion data using the following public APIs:

  • CheapShark API: Provides game deals, store comparison, price histories, and price alert registration.
  • FawazAhmed Currency API: Provides real-time currency conversion rates.

4. YOUR DATA RIGHTS & DELETION

You may delete any active price alert at any time directly in the Watchlist tab inside the LootTrack application. Deleting an alert immediately removes your registration from our active alert index.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at dirzaaulia@gmail.com or visit dirzaaulia.com.
